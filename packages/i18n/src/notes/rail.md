# rail

- From `rail.label`: The icon rail at the far left of the Canvas screen. Every item shows only an icon, so each string is both its accessible name and its tooltip.
- From `rail.artifacts.caption`: The word printed under the Artifacts icon in the 60px-wide rail, at 10px: about 5 full-width characters to a line. It is the same word as `rail.artifacts`, never a shorter synonym; when that word is longer than a line, put a \n where it splits most naturally and the caption takes two lines.
