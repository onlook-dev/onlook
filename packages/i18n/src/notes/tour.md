# tour

- From `tour.label`: First-run tour
- From `tour.scene.filtersPrompt`: The sample conversation the tour's editor shows.
- From `tour.scene.carouselsPrompt`: Typed letter by letter into a one-line prompt bubble on the tour's canvas; a sentence longer than the English wraps the bubble onto a second line. Keep it about as short as the English.
- From `tour.canvas.title`: Seven steps, each a title over a body in a 520px dialog. A body longer than two lines pushes the pager down, so keep each body to about two lines (roughly 60 full-width characters).
- From `tour.riff.body`: Selecting is the click itself; the sentence then asks the person to say what they want changed.
- From `tour.carousels.title`: Any part of a page can get variations, not just the whole page.
- From `tour.wrap.title`: The last step, an invitation to go and try things freely, not a menu item.
- From `tour.scene.layoutsPrompt`: The three prompts (`filtersPrompt`, `layoutsPrompt`, `carouselsPrompt`) are what a designer typed to the agent; write all three in one register.
